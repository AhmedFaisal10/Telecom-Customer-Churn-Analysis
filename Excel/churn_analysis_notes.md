# 📘 Churn Analysis Notes

## Objective
To understand customer churn behavior and identify the main drivers behind customer attrition in a telecom company.

---

## Overall Churn Summary
- Total Customers: 6,687
- Churned Customers: 1,796
- Overall Churn Rate: 26.86%

This indicates a relatively high churn rate, requiring targeted retention strategies.

---

## Demographic Analysis
- Senior customers show higher churn compared to younger age groups.
- Customers aged 60+ are the most vulnerable segment.

**Insight:** Age plays a significant role in churn behavior.

---

## Age Group Analysis
- Churn rate increases steadily with age.
- The highest churn rate appears in the 60+ age segments.

**Insight:** Older customers may face dissatisfaction related to pricing, service complexity, or competitor offerings.

---

## Churn Reasons
The most common churn reasons include:
- Better competitor offers
- Better devices and higher speeds from competitors
- High prices and poor service experience

**Insight:** Competitive pressure is the dominant churn driver.

---

## Competitor Churn Analysis
- Over 70% of churned customers left due to competitor-related reasons.
- Pricing and service quality are critical competitive factors.

---

## Consumption-Based Churn
- Customers with higher data consumption tend to churn more.
- High-usage customers are more price-sensitive and quality-driven.

---

## Geographic Analysis
- Certain states show significantly higher churn rates than the average.
- Geographic churn variation suggests localized competition or service quality issues.

---

## Final Takeaway
Customer churn is primarily driven by:
1. Contract type (short-term contracts)
2. High charges and high usage
3. Competitive market pressure
4. Older customer segments

Targeted retention strategies focusing on these areas can significantly reduce churn.
